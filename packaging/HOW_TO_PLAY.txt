Rule the Kingdom (the open-source port of the 2012 game by Game Insight and innoWate)

1. Put your Rule the Kingdom 5.11 APK (the Android game file, any name ending in .apk) in this
   folder, next to the game. Nothing needs to be unpacked; the update packs are already in "data".
2. Start the game:
     Windows: double-click rtk.exe
     Linux:   ./rtk   (in a terminal, from this folder)
   If the APK isn't found, the game asks you to pick it once and remembers it.

Controls: the mouse does what a finger did on the phone (click, drag to scroll, wheel or pinch to
zoom). Esc = Back, F10 = frame-rate cap (30 / 60 / display), F11 = fullscreen; both are also
buttons in the game's Settings (the gear).
Saves: Windows %APPDATA%\Rule the Kingdom, Linux ~/.local/share/Rule the Kingdom.
